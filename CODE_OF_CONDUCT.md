# Code of conduct

Participants must engage respectfully, document technical disagreements, protect confidential and participant-controlled information, and avoid overstating institutional authority or consensus.

Harassment, discrimination, coercive use proposals, retaliation, disclosure of protected evidence, and misrepresentation of participant or reviewer positions are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Project maintainers may restrict participation to protect contributors, participants, affected communities and evidence custodians.

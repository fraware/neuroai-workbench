# Release A: scientific status

**Research status as of 26 October 2026: corrective review open.**

Release A concerns the definition and measurement of the observable NeuroAI product and service landscape. The historical release-reconstruction workflow passed its mechanical checks. A later independent review identified unresolved methodological questions that prevent the historical output from serving as a final product-population estimate.

The research world-time cutoff is **24 September 2026**, and the corresponding historical knowledge-time close is **24 October 2026 at 23:59:59 UTC**. New sources observed after that close are prospective research evidence and must not be represented as historical captures.

## What has been established

- The software preserves immutable source and decision references and can reconstruct the historical Release-A analysis package.
- The measurement protocol specifies product identity, inclusion criteria, relevant temporal states, and bounded source-frame semantics.
- The historical analysis withheld a numerical estimate of the unseen product population instead of asserting an unsupported total.
- The frozen discovery and review worklist provides a defined set of outstanding evidence and adjudication tasks.

These are engineering and methodological accomplishments. A completed execution record, a verified original source and a qualified product decision are distinct forms of evidence.

## What remains under review

- **Product eligibility and currentness.** The historical registry contains six seed offering identities, while the frozen current-product predicate selected four. Currentness and lifecycle decisions for Flow FL-100 and Modius Spero remain open to evidence-bound expert review. Neither result is a final product-landscape count.
- **Resolution and denominator.** Unresolved source and offering identities affect observed counts and the interpretation of additional discovery yield.
- **Coverage and stopping.** Bounded source-search procedures and zero new resolved identities do not establish global completeness or saturation.
- **Independent scientific validation.** Product-boundary calibration, source provenance and relevant rights/authorization questions require completed review before population-level or public-release claims.

See the [product measurement contract](product-measurement-contract.md), [corrective scientific findings](release-a-corrective-review-r1.md), and [Release-A evidence review](https://github.com/fraware/neuroai-workbench/issues/424) for the documented methods and review record.

## Interpretation

Historical reconstruction success should not be cited as a passed scientific Release-A gate, a validated population denominator, market share, clinical performance, commercialization measurement, or institutional acceptance. Later publications must identify the applicable evidence cutoff, uncertainty, reviewer authority and data-rights basis.

The governing [corrective review](https://github.com/fraware/neuroai-workbench/issues/410) remains open. This status page does not authorize a canonical Observatory release, clinical or regulatory conclusions, or subsequent population estimates.

## Working boundary rule

Direct neural sensing and targeted neural intervention are in scope.

Non-neural physiological or behavioral proxy sensing alone is insufficient. A proxy-based product is in scope when the **exact product itself** implements an evidenced controlled neurological/neurocognitive state inference or closed-loop state-training function. Capability membership remains separate from evidence validity, performance, commercial state, regulatory state, deployment, and effectiveness.

This rule resolves the difficult gray-third cases without treating every biosensor, wearable, behavioral sensor, or adaptive system as NeuroAI.

The earlier PRE-G2 benchmark scaffolding remains historical implementation context; it is not the active constraint on P0.3 and Release-A work.

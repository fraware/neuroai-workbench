# Benchmark run manifest threat boundary

This PRE-G2 note covers integrity threats at the benchmark-freeze and held-out-evaluation boundary. It does not authorize a freeze, evaluation result, gate pass, publication, or assessment change.

Primary threats are substituting a different benchmark or public evaluation contract after a declared freeze; binding a freeze to the wrong D1/G1 identity; changing human dispositions without successor lineage; claiming required G2 boundary or strata coverage without controlled evidence; omitting human-review/adjudication provenance; evaluating different code, configuration, threshold, metric projection, or abstention policy than the one reported; tuning on held-out data; failing to record known benchmark exposure; treating controlled/licensed source material as redistributable; exporting item-level controlled material; and treating a structurally valid manifest as proof of scientific validity or governance approval.

The v0.2 freeze controls bind the exact `FROZEN_COMMITMENTS_ONLY` public benchmark contract digest, approved D1 digest, attributable G1 disposition, keyed membership/disposition commitments, required boundary-disposition and strata contracts, aggregate boundary counts, controlled coverage-report digests, sampling protocol, human-review provenance digest, adjudication protocol/accounting digest, exposure register, rights-review reference, and successor lineage. Human `INCLUDE`, `EXCLUDE`, and `BORDERLINE` must each have non-zero frozen coverage; human `ABSTAIN` and unresolved reviewer disagreement remain separate states.

Held-out run manifests require the exact freeze-manifest and public-contract bindings, current metric schema and `D1_INCLUDE_EXCLUDE_BINARY_V1` projection, exact Workbench/pipeline/configuration/decision-policy identities, and the declaration `HELD_OUT_NOT_USED_FOR_TUNING`. Known or unresolved contamination fails closed. PRE-G2 export is restricted to `AGGREGATE_ONLY`, and all authority-escalation fields remain false or `NONE`.

The rights-containment state deliberately says `S3_CONTROLLED_NO_REDISTRIBUTION_AUTHORITY_CLAIMED`. It is a containment statement, not a legal determination. A manifest cannot establish internal-use rights, redistribution rights, contractual compliance, or permission to place protected/licensed source bytes in S2.

Residual risks remain outside software validation. A dishonest or compromised operator can provide false aggregate counts or false evidence digests; upstream artifacts may already contain leaked held-out information; reviewers may be non-independent or use an inadequate protocol; a coverage report can be internally wrong while hashing correctly; source rights may be misclassified; and a hash binding cannot establish scientific adequacy. Operational evaluation therefore requires controlled S3 access logs, attributable human review, independent contamination/rights review where applicable, artifact provenance, and later human G2 governance.

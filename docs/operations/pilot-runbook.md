# Controlled pilot runbook

## 1. Authorise the pilot

Name the pilot owner, evidence custodians, participant or community representatives, substantive reviewers, decision authorities, confidentiality rules, retention period and incident route.

## 2. Prepare the environment

Use a dedicated workstation or controlled virtual machine. Enable encrypted storage, host access control, backups, patch management and endpoint protection outside the application.

## 3. Establish system identity

Create the case and resolve the exact system, configuration, component, population, context, jurisdiction and assessment question.

## 4. Register evidence

Preserve public or authorised bytes, access state, holder, source, version, checksum and limitations. Raw participant or neural data may remain federated.

## 5. Execute findings

Adjudicate all 78 requirements. Preserve unavailable evidence, unexecuted evaluation and demonstrated failure as different states.

## 6. Freeze and review

Assign an evidence-freeze ID, create a snapshot, verify digests and event chain, and conduct the required human or institutional review.

## 7. Issue typed decisions

Record claim adjudication, legal or regulatory status and conformance separately. Name the authority, authority basis, scope, conditions, prohibited inferences, expiry and reopening triggers.

## 8. Export and retain

Create a controlled bundle. Apply disclosure, retention and destruction rules to every exported copy.

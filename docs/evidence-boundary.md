# Evidence and decision boundary

The workbench distinguishes five questions.

1. Are the stored bytes unchanged since registration?
2. Does the assessment conform to the v4.2 data model?
3. Are references and mandatory decision objects internally consistent?
4. What is the strongest claim supported by controlled evidence?
5. Has a competent authority issued a legal, regulatory or conformance decision?

The software directly answers only the first three. Questions four and five require substantive review, evidence and named authority. A `PASS` finding is data entered by an assessor; it is not independently proven by the application.
